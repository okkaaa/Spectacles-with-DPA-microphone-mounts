# Spectacles-with-DPA-microphone-mounts
3D printable spectacles that have possibility to mount up to 17 DPA microphones in standardized positions. For assembly, 2x of the pins are needed, and 2 of the temple pieces, of which 1 mirrored. Depending on the number of desired microphones and positions, the amount of DPA mount pieces varies. Mirror the DPA mount piece to achieve different orientations, etc.
